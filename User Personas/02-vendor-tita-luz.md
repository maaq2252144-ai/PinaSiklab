# Persona 2 — Tita Luz, Small-Stall Vendor with No Storage Space

> *"Why not? … Basta good ang scheduling, kung paano pick-upin, anong oras, anong araw… kasi walang space na paglalagyan ng marami dito."*
> ("Why not? … As long as the scheduling is good: how it's picked up, what time, what day… because there's no space here to store a lot.")
> — Tita Marlene, market stall owner (interview)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Stakeholder group** | Baguio public market vendors with small stalls (food prep, peeled vegetables, small sari-sari stalls) |
| **Role in WasteLedger** | Low-volume supplier |
| **Priority** | Primary end-beneficiary (the hardest vendor group to keep) |
| **Evidence base** | Interviews with Tita Marlene (three recordings) |

## Snapshot
- **Age:** 57 · **Location:** A small stall inside Baguio City Public Market
- **Business:** Sells and prepares peeled and trimmed vegetables; very little packaging waste
- **Habits:** Brings a packed lunch from home and takes leftovers home, so there are almost no food containers or plastic
- **Tech:** Uses GCash; reachable by phone call or text
- **Outlook:** Follows the news; knows about reverse-vending machines for bottles in Manila, programs in Pasig and Makati, and SM's Trash to Cash

## Background
Tita Luz already separates three streams: **biodegradable, plastic and bottles**. Her stall produces **about 3 kg of vegetable peelings a day, at most 5 kg**. An *ubak* collector (someone who collects scraps for animal feed) picks it up **almost every day for free**. She has **no space to store waste**, and scraps **turn watery and start to smell within two days** at most.

She has strong views on who should lead. **The government**, she says, should make segregated collection happen and **provide the large drums**, because asking people to buy their own containers is a hassle.

## Goals
- **Keep the stall clear every day.** Storage is her real constraint, not money.
- **A predictable schedule:** a known time and day for pickup or drop-off
- **The government leads and supplies the containers**
- A small reward is welcome, but **convenience comes first**

## Pain points and frustrations
- **No storage, fast spoilage.** If waste isn't collected within 1–2 days, the stall smells and the waste turns to liquid.
- **Inconvenient programs fail.** *"Kung walang sasakyan… nakabihis ka… tapos punta ka SM"* ("If you don't have a car… you're dressed up… and then you have to go to SM"). People won't travel to redeem rewards.
- **Garbage collection is unreliable.** *"Ang pagkokolekta kasi ng basura hindi minsan nakukulekta"* ("Garbage sometimes just doesn't get collected").
- **Small payout.** 3–5 kg/day is **₱1.80–3.00 a day, about ₱47–78 a month**. Reaching a ₱200 payout takes about **3–4 months**.

## How she uses WasteLedger
| Step | Touchpoint | What she needs |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| Register | Gets a **small QR crate provided by the city** | No cost to her; fits her stall |
| Hand over | **Daily drop-off at a fixed time**, or the station staff collect crates on a round | A posted schedule she can rely on; nothing left more than one day |
| Get paid | Balance builds up and goes to **GCash once it reaches ₱200** | Her **balance carries over and never expires**, so slow months still count |
| Stay informed | Text or call from the station | No app, no travel |

## Design implications
- **Schedule as a feature:** a posted daily collection window, plus an optional **roving pickup** along stall rows for small suppliers. (This needs a staffing decision by the city; see Persona 6.)
- Support **small crates** and **combined weighing on a round** (one session, many small crates).
- Consider a **lower payout threshold or a monthly payout** for low-volume suppliers so the reward doesn't feel out of reach.
- Make it **easy for small stalls to say yes**: city-provided container, no paperwork, no travel.
- **Don't push out the *ubak* collector** without thinking it through. Animal feed is a higher use of food waste than compost.

## Success measures
- No waste left at the stall for more than one day
- Stays active for the whole pilot even though her payout is small

## Open questions
- Would she switch from the free *ubak* collector, and should she?
- Is roving pickup affordable for the city at small-stall volumes, or should small stalls share a crate by stall row?
