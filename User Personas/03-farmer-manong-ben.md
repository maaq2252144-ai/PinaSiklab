> *"Sayang ang tanim. Kung hindi mabenta, nabubulok lang sa trak."* ("Such a waste of the harvest. If it doesn't sell, it just rots on the truck.")

## Snapshot
- **Age:** 55 · **Location:** Atok / La Trinidad, Benguet
- **Role:** Treasurer of a 25-member highland vegetable cooperative
- **Business:** Trucks cabbage, potatoes and carrots to Baguio and La Trinidad trading posts 3–4 times a week
- **Tech:** Basic Android phone; most likely uses **GCash** (every vendor interviewed in the market does); keeps co-op records in a notebook
- **Languages:** Ilocano, Kankanaey, Filipino

## Background
Benguet farmers lose **20%–50% of their produce in marketing** (Sanchez et al., 2019). When prices crash or produce bruises in transit, vegetables that can't be sold are dumped or left to rot. Market vendors' trimmings come from the same produce. The GSO's 2025 data shows market vegetable refuse **climbing from 126 t in February to 341 t in December**, so the volumes Manong Ben's co-op sends will likely peak in the same months.

## Goals
- **Recover some income** from produce that can no longer be sold
- A **simple group payout** he can split among members without arguments
- Possibly get **compost from Irisan** back for the farms

## Pain points and frustrations
- **Selling good produce must always pay more.** He won't risk being seen as dumping vegetables that could still be sold (the concept note's pricing makes sure of this, §VI).
- **Bulk loads:** hundreds of kilos at once, not a 20 kg crate
- **Group accountability:** members need to see who contributed what
- **Timing:** arrives at dawn and can't wait in a queue

## How he uses WasteLedger
| Step | Touchpoint | What he needs |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| Register | Co-op registered as one supplier with several QR crates | Group account; optional list of members |
| Drop off | Unloads at the station after trading-post hours | Several crates logged in one session; empty-crate weight subtracted automatically |
| Get paid | Group payout to the co-op's GCash or bank account | **Breakdown per drop-off** for the co-op meeting; **the balance never expires** |
| Non-cash option | Compost from Irisan instead of cash | Clear conversion rates |

## Design implications
- **Group/co-op supplier type** with an optional breakdown by member
- **Batch weighing mode** for station staff
- **Monthly statement** (SMS summary plus a printable PDF)
- **Separate weekly cap for co-ops**, so they aren't capped at a single vendor's level

## Success measures
- One co-op active during the pilot
- Members say the payout and the split are fair
- No sellable produce diverted (spot-checked through grading)
